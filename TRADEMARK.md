**You may always** say truthfully what your skills do — that they are a fork of the NxisAI agent
skills, that they work with NxisAI, or that they talk to the NxisAI API. Nominative use of a
trademark to describe a relationship accurately is not something we would object to, and not
something trademark law prevents.
